Lama — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026 · Version 1.1 (previous version: 19 September 2026)

Lama is an iPhone app that reads your EPUB books aloud and answers questions about what you have heard so far. This policy explains what leaves your device, where it goes, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it. It is written to match what the app actually does.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Natalia Mitina, an individual developer. Contact: nmitina91@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • Lama has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not know who you are.
  • Your books stay on your iPhone. The whole book, your library, contacts and location are never sent anywhere.
  • Cloud features (the assistant and the cloud voice) send small pieces of text to our server and on to AI providers, only after you agree on the “Cloud features” screen. You can turn them off at any time in Profile; the built‑in iPhone voice keeps reading either way.
  • Our server stores nothing you send. It forwards the request and forgets it.

3. Where it goes

Cloud requests travel over HTTPS to the Lama server (lama-proxy-production.up.railway.app), hosted by Railway in the European Union (Amsterdam region). The server then forwards the text to the following processors:

RecipientWhatTheir policy
Anthropic (Claude models)Your question and the excerpts, to generate the answerPrivacy Policy, Commercial Terms
OpenRouterThe text of the passage, routed to the speech‑model vendorPrivacy Policy
Alibaba Cloud (Qwen speech model, via OpenRouter)The text of the passage, to synthesize the audioPrivacy Policy
AppleYour voice recording, only when on‑device recognition is not available for the languageApple Privacy; Apple’s speech recognition is governed by iOS

The AI model and voice are chosen on our server. If we switch to a different provider, we will update this policy and the app will ask for your consent again.

4. How long it is kept

  • Our server: nothing is stored — not books, not questions, not audio. Logs contain only technical identifiers, status codes and sizes, never text.
  • On your iPhone: the history of questions and answers, and a cache of already‑synthesized audio. You can delete history entries in History, clear the voice cache in voice settings, and remove everything by deleting the app. Nothing is backed up to iCloud by the audio cache.
  • Anthropic: API inputs and outputs are not used to train models and are retained for a limited period under Anthropic’s Commercial Terms.
  • OpenRouter: prompt storage is opt‑in and off by default; we keep both optional settings (“Private Input & Output Logging” and “OpenRouter Use of Inputs/Outputs”) off for our account, so OpenRouter retains only request metadata such as token counts and latency, not the text.
  • Alibaba Cloud (the speech model): according to OpenRouter’s published provider data policies, this provider does not train on prompts, and the period for which it retains them is not stated. What we send there is a passage of the book you are listening to — never your question, your library or anything identifying you.
  • Apple: per Apple’s speech‑recognition terms shown in iOS.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Cloud features are off until you agree, and you can switch them off at any time in Profile → Cloud features. New requests stop immediately.
  • You can delete history entries and the voice cache inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything.
  • For users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom: the legal basis for cloud processing is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw as described above. You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we hold no data about you, most requests are satisfied by the in‑app controls; for anything else, write to us.
  • For users in Russia: processing is based on your consent under Federal Law No. 152‑FZ; the same in‑app controls and contact apply.
  • Questions or requests: nmitina91@gmail.com.

6. Children

Lama is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly process data of minors.

7. What Lama does not do

No user accounts. No analytics or crash‑reporting SDKs. No advertising. No tracking across apps or websites (the app declares NSPrivacyTracking = false). No selling or sharing of data for marketing.

8. Changes

Updates are published at this address with a new effective date. If a change adds a new recipient of your text, the app will show the consent screen again before sending anything.

4. Сколько хранится

  • Наш сервер: ничего не хранится — ни книги, ни вопросы, ни аудио. В логах только технические идентификаторы, коды и размеры, никогда — текст.
  • На вашем iPhone: история вопросов и ответов и кеш уже синтезированного аудио. Записи истории удаляются в разделе «История», кеш голоса — в настройках голоса; удаление приложения удаляет всё. Кеш аудио не попадает в резервные копии iCloud.
  • Anthropic: данные API не используются для обучения моделей и хранятся ограниченное время согласно Commercial Terms Anthropic.
  • OpenRouter: хранение запросов включается только явно и по умолчанию выключено; обе необязательные настройки («Private Input & Output Logging» и «OpenRouter Use of Inputs/Outputs») в нашем аккаунте выключены, поэтому OpenRouter сохраняет только метаданные запроса (число токенов, задержка), а не текст.
  • Alibaba Cloud (речевая модель): по опубликованной OpenRouter таблице политик провайдеров, этот поставщик не обучается на запросах, а срок их хранения не указан. Туда уходит отрывок книги, которую вы слушаете, — никогда ваш вопрос, библиотека или что-либо, идентифицирующее вас.
  • Apple: по условиям распознавания речи Apple, показанным в iOS.
